## Human verification checklist

1. Open `/` at a desktop viewport of at least 1440×900.
   - Expected: the headline, supporting copy, and calls to action remain clear
     over the dark left-side text-safe area.
2. Open `/` at 375×812.
   - Expected: there is no horizontal scroll and the building's focal point
     remains recognizable within the right-biased crop.
3. Allow the typewriter introduction to complete.
   - Expected: the poster transitions to the video without a bright flash or
     visible layout shift.
4. Watch two complete loops in a Chromium browser.
   - Expected: motion stays restrained and the final-to-first-frame join is
     unobtrusive.
5. Test Safari or another browser using the MP4 fallback.
   - Expected: the same composition plays without a broken source.
6. Enable reduced motion, then reload `/`.
   - Expected: the static poster remains visible and video motion is not used.
7. Enable data-saving mode or simulate a slow 2G connection.
   - Expected: the poster remains usable without downloading or revealing the
     video.
8. Confirm the asset-generation record contains the provider, generation date,
   account owner, and commercial-usage permission.
   - Expected: usage rights are documented before closure.
